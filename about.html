I'm Lynn

My name is Lynn Nijhuis. I am a student from the Netherlands, currently studying Creative Media and Game Technology at the Saxion university of applied sciences. I am also doing a content creator internship at the municipality of Enschede, to encourage youth participation through means of video.

I have many years of experience in 2D art, and have been actively branching out to include animation and video in my skillset. I generally love being creative and trying new things, even in my free time. Whether that's picking up a new type of art supply, or a new activity altogether.
