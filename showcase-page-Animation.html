Animation reel - princess

Animation - guard character

In the other animation project, I went into depth on why I like animation, which is why I will leave out that context for now. Jut

I loved this class! It gave me an outlet for the animation itch I had been feeling my whole life.

Character wise, I wanted to create a tidy princess character, who walks with her hands folded, and is overall quite posh and a bit arrogant. I think I portrayed that well in the animation reel. I took inspiration from other princess characters and filmed the reference footage of it myself.

What I learned from this project:

First and foremost, I learned how to animate in Maya, which is something that I had never done before. I also learned how to utilize curves and how to make your animation look smoother by using them. Translating a personality idea into how a character would behave and move, was also a difficult task, but not something I couldn't do. Shooting the reference footage was actually quite easy! And lastly, I now know how to implement self-made animations into Unity.

The rig given to us might have been a bit buggy, but it was nothing I couldn't manage. All in all, I really adored this class!
