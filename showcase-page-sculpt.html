Dracula Sculpt

Dracula sculpt

For my sculpting class, I was tasked to design a character, and make a 3D sculpt of that design in ZBrush.

To gather inspiration for your character, you were also allowed to pick characters from literature that did not have a set design. So, I picked a character from one of my favorite pieces of old literature, which was Count Dracula himself. Based on a lot of research consisting of analyzing versions of Dracula across media, I landed on this design. It was then time to make it into an actual character.

This was my first time using 3D sculpting software, but I picked it up pretty fast!

What I learned from this project:

I learned how to use a new application, which is ZBrush. I also learned how to portray a 2D character design in a 3D space. Retopolizing was also something I didn't know how to do beforehand, but I picked it up very fast, and my model was rig-ready!

I had a great time working on something new, and portray my character designs in a new kind of way! I will definitely sculpt again.
