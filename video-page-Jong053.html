JONG053

My internship at JONG053

JONG053 is a project within the municipality of Enschede that aspires to promote youth participation within Enschede. They make videos that inform the youth about upcoming events they're hosting to promote this, inform them about interesting subjects overall, or get opinions from youth while out and about during street interviews, for example.

I get to make those videos.

My job within JONG053 is to manage their social media, and create content for their page. I also have other duties, such as giving class presentations, but my main job is indeed content creation. A lot of the videos I've made just far are displayed down here. They are in Dutch, so I will give context where needed.

As of right now, I am currently still in this internship. I have been having a great time thus far and I keep learning new things! For now, here are some of the videos I have made in the past 2 months.

Street interviews - Democracy day

What I did: Script, direct, film and edit

For this video, we went out on the streets to ask youth about their opinions on the new plans the municipality has made. More specifically, what their most important topic was and why.

The video itself had to be upbeat, and in a simple, understandable and entertaining way for the youth. Some creative choices I made for this video was showing the subject the person was talking about on screen, so that there would be no confusion, since the interviewees would be talking about different subjects. I also edited out a lot. The young man in purple was talking for a very long while, so I had to cut it a certain way to make it entertaining. Fast shots are the way to go, which is what I kept up throughout the video.

The biggest challenge of this video, was directing the youth. One of the interviewers had never done this before, but with some support and good direction, he made it through his first interview, and was even excited to do another one, despite us already having enough content!

Call to action: Youth participation questionnaire

What I did: Script, direct and edit

The purpose of this video was to promote the ongoing youth participation questionnaire hosted by Kennispunt Twente. We wanted to garner as many opinions as possible, which is why we made a reel to promote the matter.

This video had to grab attention, which is why I chose for the dramatic turnaround and zoom in at the beginning to keep people from scrolling away immediately.

The call to action had to be short and concise, which is why I made this video very short and a quick watch, but with all the needed information. We filmed in the boardroom, which made for some interesting composition shots, especially at the end.

I actually encountered no challenges during this video! The youth team members were very quick and understood what I wanted to do. If anything, that understanding made that I could play around with camera angles a bit more. It's very rare we get to be able to have two people speak in one take, so I'm glad I could avoid harsh cuts for thos video, which made it so much smoother. Definitely one of my favorites to work on.

The mayor explains about the Youth Climate Action fund

What I did: be in front of the camera, direct and edit

Sometimes, I have to be in front of the camera as well during my internship. This was one of those occasions, where I got the opportunity to film with the mayor for an explanation of the Youth Climate Action Fund that Enschede was chosen for. It's a project where youngsters in Enschede with good ideas can realize their ideas to make Enschede greener with an actual budget.

Naturally, we were tasked with promoting it. We arranged a meeting with the mayor who was able to do a talk about the project.

This was my first time in front of the camera, which made me have a feel on what the youth team was experiencing. Though, given my history with being in front of crowds, it came quite natural to me. I had an excellent youth team member who filmed, but who still made sure I was the lead director, even in front of the camera.

The biggest challenge of this video was to direct an important figure. We had the mayor's adviser with us, who made sure everything looked fine. It was a bit different than I was used to, but once I (and the camerawoman) got past the barrier, they were both very kind and actively thinking along!

Call to action: Youth Climate Action Fund

What I did: Script, direct, film and edit

Another video as promotion of the aforementioned Youth Climate Action fund! This was the first video we made to promote the subject.

This video contained lots of information. To make it more digestible, I made the creative choice to let different people say the bits of information, switched locations, and occasionally did voice-overs. That way, the video wasn't focussed on one thing for too long, and way more watchable.

The biggest challenge of this video was to direct a bigger group of youth. They had a tendency to get distracted. Though, I kept them all in check, and we got through the filming process before our next appointment. They were all thinking along as well, but kept my wishes in mind. A great, talented team to work with.

As of right now, my internship is still ongoing, which means my catalogue of videos will keep growing, and I will keep developing my content creation based skills. I am very excited to continue pursuing content creation with this team!
