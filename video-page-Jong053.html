JONG053

My internship at JONG053

JONG053 is a project within the municipality of Enschede that aspires to promote youth participation within Enschede. They make videos that inform the youth about upcoming events they're hosting to promote this, inform them about interesting subjects overall, or get opinions from youth while out and about during street interviews, for example.

I get to make those videos.

My job within JONG053 is to manage their social media, and create content for their page. I also have other duties, such as giving class presentations, but my main job is indeed content creation. A lot of the videos I've made just far are displayed down here. They are in Dutch, so I will give context where needed.

As of right now, I am currently still in this internship. I have been having a great time thus far and I keep learning new things!

Street interviews - Democracy day

This is a video I designed, directed and filmed for democracy day. For this video, we went out on the streets to ask youth about their opinions on the new plans the municipality has made. More specifically, what their most important topic was and why. The video itself had to be upbeat, and in a simple, understandable and entertaining way for the youth. Some creative choices I made for this video was showing the subject the person was talking about on screen, so that there would be no confusion, since the interviewees would be talking about different subjects. I also edited out a lot. The young man in purple was talking for a very long while, so I had to cut it a certain way to make it entertaining. Fast shots are the way to go, which is what I kept up throughout the video. The biggest challenge of this video, was directing the youth. One of the interviewers had never done this before, but with some support and good direction, he made it through his first interview, and was even excited to do another one, despite us already having enough content!

The Hémennekes of Aamsveen - game trailer i have free will so i can make this so lang as i want aaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaa

The Hémennekes are a Dutch tale based in my region, Twente. They are mischievous, gome-like will-o-wisps, who cause chaos. This tale was the topic for my fourth group project, where we had to make a retelling of an old tale from Twente.

I was, amongst other things, tasked with editing the trailer. This was the first time I edited a horror trailer, so I did my due diligence in researching how other games do it by looking up indie-game examples in a similar genre.

What I learned from editing this video:

★ Horror must build suspense. Starting with a mysterious monologue does achieve that! ★ Horror games play with audio a lot. I tried to during this trailer, which I achieved with the monologue, but based on the limitations of the audio given to me, it was not possible. ★ I really enjoyed creating the suspense!

All in all, with it being my first time editing in the horror genre, my team and the general audience loved it. Some of the people at the event our game was showcased at, even stood and looked at the trailer when the computer was in idle mode! I would definitely tackle something like this again.
