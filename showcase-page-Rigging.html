Animation - guard

Animation - guard character

In the other animation project, I went into depth on why I like animation, which is why I will leave out that context for now. Just know that I do!

For this character, I wanted to create something entirely different than a princess. I wanted to create the opposite, which was a guard, knight, whatever you wish to call it. That means the character had to be, well, on guard, look stern, and alert.

This time, I worked with a more difficult rig, which had more elements to control. That means I could tweak the facial expression and hair as well. The latter gave me some issues, since hair physics is something you don't get in a day, but what I ended up with, was, according to my peers, 'not bad'. The facial expressions really added to the character, which is why I'm glad it was available to me.

What I learned from this project:

I showed that I could work different rigs by picking a more difficult one, which included the face rig. I had fun working with that! I also learned how to work with props, and how to link it to the character. The aforementioned hair physics where also something I'm learning to understand! I had fun dabbling in animation again, and I would love to do it more.
